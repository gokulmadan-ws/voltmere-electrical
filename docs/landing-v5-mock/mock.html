Frequently asked questions

Which charger is right for my car?

Most home chargers suit most electric and plug-in hybrid cars, but the best choice depends on your vehicle, how you drive and your preferences. We will help you weigh up the options once we know a little about your setup.

Do I need a driveway or private parking?

A charger needs a suitable parking position and a safe route for the cable that does not create a hazard. We assess this for your property rather than assuming it. Running a cable across a public pavement is not a standard solution, so let us know your setup and we will advise.

How much will installation cost?

It depends on the charger you choose, the cable route and any electrical work your property needs. We give you a clear quote once we understand your requirements, rather than a headline figure that changes later.

How long does installation take?

Timing varies with the property and the scope of work involved. Once the team has reviewed your enquiry, they can talk you through what to expect for your specific installation.

Can I have a charger installed before my car arrives?

Many homeowners plan the installation while waiting for their car. Tell us where you are in the process and we will suggest sensible timing so your charger is ready when you need it.

What happens after I enquire?

The team reviews your details and contacts you to discuss your installation and the next steps. Submitting the form is an enquiry, not a booking, and it does not produce an instant final price.
